Inspect this sequence of timestamped frames from a staged cashier workplace. The upright grey metal storage cabinet next to the desk represents the cash safe. The glass room door is the access door. The employee side is where a person sits or works at the desk with the laptop and cash-handling equipment.

Look for these three visible events:
1. safe_left_open: the metal cash cabinet door remains OPEN while the employee has moved away from the cabinet or returned to the desk. An open door during active access alone is normal. A closed door is not a violation.
2. access_door_left_open: a person has passed through the room entrance and moved away, but the access door remains OPEN. A door being opened only to pass through and then closed is normal.
3. outerwear_in_cash_area: the employee enters or works on the staff side wearing an outdoor jacket or coat. No cash transaction is required. A blouse, shirt, business blazer or a garment carried in the hand is not outdoor clothing being worn. A customer on the opposite side is not the employee.
